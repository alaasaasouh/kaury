006 / Our Story

Two Decades
of Devotion

KAURY began in a small roastery in 2009 — one woman, one drum roaster, and an obsession with the perfect cup. What started as a pursuit of personal excellence quietly became a movement.

Today, our atelier operates from its original address. The drum still turns. The obsession has only deepened. We source fewer than forty lots per year — each chosen for its singular character, each roasted with the same care as the first batch fifteen years ago.

We do not chase volume. We chase moments — the first sip of a natural Ethiopian, the quiet intensity of a washed Yemeni, the fruit-forward brightness of a Colombian nano-lot. These are not just coffees. They are experiences designed to stop you mid-thought.

01

Craft Over Scale

We roast every batch by hand, never exceeding 12kg per drum. When demand exceeds capacity, we open a waitlist — not a second shift.

02

Radical Traceability

Every bag ships with the farm name, harvest date, process method, and the name of the person who picked it. You deserve to know your coffee's full story.

03

Fair at the Source

We pay a minimum of three times the commodity price, directly to farmers. No middlemen. No hollow certifications. Just a fair relationship built on respect.
